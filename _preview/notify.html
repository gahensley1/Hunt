Notifications

You asked to move the request earlier and explain what it buys. Two problems to settle first, then three places to put it.

1. The app already asks — cold, with no explanation. openRoster() fires Notification.requestPermission() the moment a builder opens any roster. No card, no reason given, just the browser’s grey box.

A refusal is permanent. Once someone taps Block, the app can never ask again on that device — only they can undo it, buried in site settings. Asking cold is the single most reliable way to lose the permission forever.

2. Right now the permission buys almost nothing. Both notifications use new Notification(…), which only fires while the app is open on screen. There is no pushManager, no subscription, no VAPID key — no background delivery of any kind.

So a builder who closes the app is told nothing. And on iPhone new Notification() is not supported at all — today the feature is Android-and-desktop-only, and only while watching.
