The notification ask, as a toast

Shown on the Case Ready screen, where the card sits today. All three are the app's existing toast — ink pill, paper text, 30px radius — but tappable and staying put until answered, because the browser only raises its own prompt from a tap. The bordered brass card is retired in every one of them. Copy below is a placeholder in your voice; you set the final wording.
