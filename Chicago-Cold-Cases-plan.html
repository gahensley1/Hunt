The Millennium Park Enquiry

CASE No. 606001  ·  Millennium Park  ·  CHICAGO 60602  ·  41.8826, -87.6226  ·  DIFFICULTY 2  ·  FREE

Twenty-five acres laid over a railyard — a mirrored drop, two speaking towers, and a bandstand of curling steel. All within a quarter-mile.

1
The Silver Drop find a picture
The Silver Drop — A vast bean of polished steel that swallows the skyline and hands it back bent. Photograph it whole.
Hint: Stand well back on the upper plaza; the whole drop will not fit from close to.
2
The Chamber Beneath find a picture
The Chamber Beneath — Step under the great drop and look up into the whorl, where a detective is reflected without end.
Hint: Walk directly underneath and point the lens straight up.
3
The Speaking Towers find a picture
The Speaking Towers — Two glass columns face one another across the stone, each wearing the portrait of a stranger.
Hint: South-west of the great drop, across Monroe. They change face every few minutes.
4
The Sheet of Water find a picture
The Sheet of Water — Between the towers lies a black granite floor kept under a skin of water. Record it.
Hint: Photograph from the end, low down, and the towers will double in the wet stone.
5
The Ribbon Bandstand find a picture
The Ribbon Bandstand — A stage crowned with great curling leaves of brushed steel, as though a letter had been unfolded and left standing.
Hint: North-east corner, facing the lawn. The whole crown reads best from the rear of the seating.
6
The Iron Canopy find a picture
The Iron Canopy — Above the lawn a web of steel pipes crosses the open air, carrying sound where a roof would be.
Hint: Stand on the grass and photograph upward into the lattice.
7
The Winding Bridge find a picture
The Winding Bridge — A serpent in steel scales carries walkers over the roaring road below. Capture its curve.
Hint: It leaves the bandstand's lawn on the east side. The curve shows from the middle.
8
The Ring of Columns find a picture
The Ring of Columns — A half-circle of Doric columns stands at the north-west corner — a Greek temple that never got its roof.
Hint: Wrigley Square, at Michigan and Randolph.
9
The Roll of Benefactors find a picture
The Roll of Benefactors — Names cut into stone at the foot of the columns: the subscribers who paid for this ground.
Hint: Read the base of the peristyle, on the park side.
10
The Shoulder Hedge find a picture
The Shoulder Hedge — A great clipped wall of green, planted to shelter a garden from the wind and from the eye.
Hint: The south-east garden. The hedge is taller than a man.
11
The Seam of Water find a picture
The Seam of Water — A narrow channel runs beside the boardwalk where visitors trail their fingers. Capture it.
Hint: Inside the same garden, along the diagonal walk.
12
The Gangplank find a picture
The Gangplank — A bridge like a ship's ramp carries you over Monroe Street to the gallery's upper floor.
Hint: The south end of the park, pointing at the Art Institute.

The Buckingham Waterworks

CASE No. 606002  ·  Buckingham Fountain & Grant Park  ·  CHICAGO 60605  ·  41.8758, -87.6189  ·  DIFFICULTY 2  ·  FREE

A great rose-marble basin at the heart of the lakefront park, guarded by bronze horses of the sea. A quarter-mile of formal walks about it.

1
The Tiered Basin find a picture
The Tiered Basin — Three great dishes of rose marble, each smaller than the last, standing in a pool wider than a house. Photograph the whole work.
Hint: Approach from the east walk; the full tier line shows best from the lake side.
2
The Bronze Sea-Horses find a picture
The Bronze Sea-Horses — Four pairs of horses rear in the water, one pair for each state that touches this inland sea.
Hint: They sit at the four compass points of the outer basin.
3
The Great Column find a picture
The Great Column — On the hour the centre throws a column of water higher than the surrounding trees. Wait for it.
Hint: Displays run on the hour through the warm months; stand upwind.
4
The Formal Beds find a picture
The Formal Beds — Beds of roses laid out in pattern to the north and south, kept in strict order.
Hint: Either side of the fountain plaza, on the axis.
5
The Rider Without His Bow find a picture
The Rider Without His Bow — A mounted figure draws a weapon that is not there; the sculptor left it to the imagination.
Hint: West of the fountain at the Congress entrance, north side.
6
The Rider Without His Spear find a picture
The Rider Without His Spear — His companion raises an arm to cast a shaft that was never carved.
Hint: The matching plinth on the south side of the same entrance.
7
The Stone Rail find a picture
The Stone Rail — A balustrade of pale stone rings the water, broken by urns at intervals.
Hint: Anywhere on the perimeter; frame two urns for the pattern.
8
The Coloured Lamps find a picture
The Coloured Lamps — Lamps set below the waterline dress the spray in colour once the light goes.
Hint: After dusk only. By day, record the lamp housings in the basin wall.
9
The Radiating Walks find a picture
The Radiating Walks — Paths leave the basin like spokes from a hub. Stand at the head of one and photograph the line.
Hint: Any of the four axes; the eastern walk is the longest.
10
The Elm Avenue find a picture
The Elm Avenue — A double file of trees marches north and south, planted to frame the water.
Hint: The allees run parallel to Columbus Drive.
11
The Benefactress find a picture
The Benefactress — A plaque records the lady who gave this fountain in her brother's memory.
Hint: Set into the plaza stonework near the west approach.
12
The Open Stage find a picture
The Open Stage — A great shell of a stage stands north of the fountain, built for music without walls.
Hint: North end of the park, past Monroe.

The Cultural Hall Investigation

CASE No. 606003  ·  Chicago Cultural Center  ·  CHICAGO 60602  ·  41.8837, -87.6248  ·  DIFFICULTY 1  ·  FREE

A public palace of marble and coloured glass, raised as the city's library and never charging a penny at the door. Twelve findings under two great domes - and not a drop of rain among them.

1
The Great Glass Dome find a picture
The Great Glass Dome — A dome of many thousand pieces of coloured glass, the largest of its kind anywhere. Photograph it from beneath.
Hint: Third floor, the south hall. Free entry; the lift saves the stairs.
2
The Signs of the Heavens find a picture
The Signs of the Heavens — Set within that dome are the signs the ancients read in the sky. Find one and record it.
Hint: Look to the ring of the dome rather than its centre.
3
The Second Dome find a picture
The Second Dome — A smaller dome of green and amber glass crowns the northern hall, built to honour old soldiers.
Hint: North end, second floor. Quieter than its famous sister.
4
The Marble Stair find a picture
The Marble Stair — A staircase of white Carrara, wide enough for a regiment, turning beneath vaults of glass mosaic.
Hint: Either end of the building; the south stair is the grander.
5
The Mother-of-Pearl find a picture
The Mother-of-Pearl — Look closely at the mosaic: among the glass and gold are set shells that catch the light.
Hint: On the stair vaults and arch soffits. Come close; the effect is lost at distance.
6
The Inscriptions find a picture
The Inscriptions — The walls carry the names and sayings of authors, cut in stone and set in many tongues.
Hint: Along the stair walls and above the arches.
7
The Bronze Standards find a picture
The Bronze Standards — Lamps of bronze stand at the turns of the stair like sentries.
Hint: At the newel posts.
8
The Soldiers' Relics find a picture
The Soldiers' Relics — In the northern hall are kept the arms and colours of men who marched in the great American war.
Hint: The Memorial Hall, second floor north.
9
The Arched Doorway find a picture
The Arched Doorway — A doorway of carved stone opens on the street that carries the first President's name.
Hint: The Washington Street entrance.
10
The Mosaic Floor find a picture
The Mosaic Floor — Underfoot in the lobby, small tiles are laid in pattern. Photograph a yard of it.
Hint: Both ground-floor lobbies are laid this way.
11
The Free Gallery find a picture
The Free Gallery — Rooms of pictures that ask no admission - the city's oldest promise to the poor.
Hint: Any exhibition room; the contents change, the promise does not.
12
The Room of Music find a picture
The Room of Music — A hall where players perform at midday to any who wander in.
Hint: Ask at the desk which room is in use today.

The Wooded Island Affair

CASE No. 606004  ·  The Wooded Island & Osaka Garden  ·  CHICAGO 60637  ·  41.7897, -87.582  ·  DIFFICULTY 2  ·  FREE

An island in a lagoon, made ground for the great fair of 1893 and planted since as a sanctuary. A half-mile loop, and a garden given by a city across the sea.

1
The Garden Gate find a picture
The Garden Gate — A gate of timber and tile marks where one country's garden begins inside another's park.
Hint: North end of the island, reached by the footbridge.
2
The Moon Bridge find a picture
The Moon Bridge — A bridge arched so steeply that its reflection completes a circle in still water.
Hint: Inside the garden; wait for the water to settle.
3
The Stone Lantern find a picture
The Stone Lantern — A lantern carved in stone stands where no lamp is needed, keeping a custom rather than a flame.
Hint: Beside the path near the water's edge.
4
The Falling Water find a picture
The Falling Water — Water steps down over set stones with a sound arranged as carefully as the rocks.
Hint: Follow the sound from the garden's upper path.
5
The Trained Pine find a picture
The Trained Pine — A pine has been drawn out over the water by patient hands, held in a shape no wind gave it.
Hint: Overhanging the pond; look for the props.
6
The Stepping Stones find a picture
The Stepping Stones — Flat stones cross shallow water, set apart by the length of a walker's stride.
Hint: Take them slowly; they are older than they look.
7
The Lagoon find a picture
The Lagoon — Still water rings the island on every side, dug for the fair and never filled in.
Hint: Any open bank will serve.
8
The Sanctuary Notice find a picture
The Sanctuary Notice — A board asks visitors to keep to the paths, for the birds' sake. Record it.
Hint: At either bridge, where the island is entered.
9
The Fairground Marker find a picture
The Fairground Marker — Something on this ground records the exposition of 1893, whose White City stood here.
Hint: Read the plaques near the bridges.
10
The Second Crossing find a picture
The Second Crossing — The island is reached by two bridges; find the one that is not the way you came in.
Hint: South end, toward the meadow.
11
The Meadow find a picture
The Meadow — At the island's heart is open grass and tall planting, left rough on purpose.
Hint: The central clearing.
12
The Heron's Water find a picture
The Heron's Water — Somewhere along the bank the water runs shallow enough for a long-legged fisherman. Photograph the spot, bird or no bird.
Hint: The quiet east bank, early or late.

The Pullman Enquiry

CASE No. 606005  ·  Pullman National Historical Park  ·  CHICAGO 60628  ·  41.6893, -87.6086  ·  DIFFICULTY 2  ·  FREE

A company town of 1880, built entire by one man for the workers of his sleeping-car works - and now a national park. Twelve findings in half a mile of brick.

1
The Clock Tower find a picture
The Clock Tower — A tower of brick rises above the old works, with a clock set high where every hand could see it.
Hint: The administration building, at 111th Street.
2
The Company Hotel find a picture
The Company Hotel — A grand hotel in the Queen Anne manner, built for the visitors of a town that had no other.
Hint: Corner of 111th and Forrestville, opposite the works.
3
The Green Church find a picture
The Green Church — A church built of a stone so green it might be weathered copper - but it is the rock itself.
Hint: Corner of 112th and St Lawrence.
4
The Workers' Rows find a picture
The Workers' Rows — Terraces of brick houses, each family's door a little different from its neighbour's.
Hint: Any residential street east of Cottage Grove.
5
The Painted Doors find a picture
The Painted Doors — Look along a row and record where the colours change from house to house.
Hint: Best on the east side, where the rows run longest.
6
The Works Gate find a picture
The Works Gate — The entrance through which the men walked to build railway carriages for the world.
Hint: The north end of the administration building.
7
The Market Square find a picture
The Market Square — A place set aside for the town's trade, arranged as a circle rather than a street.
Hint: North on Champlain, at 112th.
8
The Overseer's House find a picture
The Overseer's House — Larger houses stood for the men who gave the orders; find one and mark how it differs.
Hint: The corner plots are the giveaway.
9
The Park Notice find a picture
The Park Notice — A sign records that this ground is now held for the nation. Photograph it.
Hint: At the visitor centre.
10
The Stables find a picture
The Stables — Buildings raised for horses, when horses were the engines of a town.
Hint: South of the market square.
11
The Arched Windows find a picture
The Arched Windows — Somewhere in the brick is an arch of a shape you have not yet recorded. Find it.
Hint: The public buildings carry the finest brickwork.
12
The Planted Street find a picture
The Planted Street — The town was laid out with trees as deliberately as with drains. Record an avenue.
Hint: Any street running north and south.
